What this is

The figure is a low-poly anatomical model, not a scan of you. Regions are a way to group the data; the shape itself carries no measurement. Arms sit with the chest because the watch reads your pulse at the wrist.

The record

Every reading you import is kept in this browser, and the range control only changes what is shown. Each new export is merged in, so "All" grows over time.
